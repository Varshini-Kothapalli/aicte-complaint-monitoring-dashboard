## Provenance Guidelines

All data consumed or generated by the system is tagged with one of four immutable provenance labels:

1. **`VERIFIED`**: Validated against authoritative master records.
2. **`IMPORTED_UNVERIFIED`**: Real-world historical imports whose current institutional status is unverified (all ACSS records).
3. **`SYNTHETIC`**: Artificially generated demonstration records. Synthetic entities use `SYN-` prefixes, letter-triplet institution names (e.g., `ABC Engineering College`), and emails ending in `@demo.invalid`. **Synthetic data must never be presented as real historical facts.**
4. **`DERIVED`**: Computed by application algorithms (similarity scores, vector embeddings, overdue indicators).

## Protection and Privacy Notice

- Raw historical data in `data/raw/` contains unredacted citizen and officer information. It is excluded from version control under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
- Processed CSV files in `data/processed/extracted/csv/` are sanitized and serve as reproducible fixtures for database seeding.
- For complete field descriptions, data distributions, and quality analysis, see `docs/DATASET_ASSESSMENT.md`.
